## 8. Do / Don't
- Do: lead with the concrete outcome (real/complete price), list the actual modes and cost elements, answer FAQs with "Yes." + one clarifying sentence, keep sentences short (observed on [P1]).
- Don't: hype words, superlatives without proof, exclamation marks, "seamless/nahtlos", "intelligent platform" (house bans + observed off-notes), invented customers or numbers.
- Always: claims only from POSITIONING.md proof (with source); otherwise `[NEED: proof]`.
- Never: mention or allude to the predecessor product, its name, or that Multimodalis is a newer version of it (Saad, chat, 2026-10-09). Copy must stand on its own as Multimodalis copy.
