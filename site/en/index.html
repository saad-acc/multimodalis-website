All Transport Modes. One Pricing Logic.

Multimodalis connects pricing structures that usually live in separate spreadsheets, separate teams and separate systems.

01/ 04

Ocean

FCL and LCL with port locals — contract port-to-port plus live carrier online rates on the same lanes. FAK, named-account, basket, hazardous and special-equipment contracts; FCL uploads checked cell by cell; door-to-door from real inland tariffs, not estimated add-ons.

Road

FTL and LTL including toll, fuel, pickup and drop-off. Each carrier's own chargeable-weight rules (volume, loading metre, pallet) across all rule types and all surcharge types; out-of-limit shipments show “on request”, not a misleading price.

Intermodal

Truck, rail/truck and barge/truck options for one container on one screen, one-way and roundtrip. Toll-aware distance pricing, recalculated when a toll change takes effect; terminal costs, KV / all-in zone surcharges with period control; Rhine water levels monitored against each supplier's low-water band.

Airfreight

Chargeable weight per airline divisor and rounding, FSC and SSC, security fees and add-ons in the landed price. Every higher weight break is checked automatically and the saving shown; a piece-restriction engine tests each piece.

From price to offer and capacity

Offers and capacity requests use the same calculated prices as every query — sales, procurement and pricing work from one version of the truth.

Sales offer generator

  • Markup rules per customer group and cost item, applied automatically
  • Approval flow: draft, review, approval, sent, archived
  • Sent versions are frozen, with stored proof of the buying price
  • Branded PDFs with your colours, logo and terms
  • Price-update wizard emails updated offers to every affected customer
  • AI inquiry analysis turns a customer email into a structured inquiry with a checklist of missing details. It pre-fills the offer; it does not set prices.

Capacity requests

  • Capacity requests built directly from quote results
  • Suppliers answer via yes/no links in the email, no login required
  • Award in Multimodalis: the winner is notified, the others are released automatically
  • Invite new suppliers without disclosing prices
  • Capacity KPI view with pipeline value and trends
