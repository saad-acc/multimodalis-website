Privacy Policy

The German version (Datenschutz) is the legally relevant one. This English text is a translation.

1. Controller

Further details will follow once incorporation is complete.

2. Hosting

This website is served as a static site via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit the site, GitHub processes technical server log data (in particular IP address, time of access, requested URL, user agent and referrer) that is necessary to deliver the pages. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and reliable provision of the website). Further information: GitHub General Privacy Statement. GitHub, Inc. participates in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework; see the Data Privacy Framework list.

3. Contact

The demo request form on this website is currently inactive and does not transmit any data. If you contact us by other means (for example by email once a contact address is published), we process the personal data you provide solely to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR or, where the request aims at entering into a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We delete the data when it is no longer needed for that purpose, unless statutory retention periods apply.

4. Your rights

You have the right:

  1. To request information about your personal data processed by us (Art. 15 GDPR).
  2. To request correction of incorrect or completion of your personal data stored by us (Art. 16 GDPR).
  3. To request deletion of your personal data stored by us, unless the processing is necessary to fulfill a legal obligation or to assert, exercise or defend legal claims (Art. 17 GDPR).
  4. To request restriction of the processing of your personal data (Art. 18 GDPR).
  5. To receive your personal data in a structured, common and machine-readable format or to request transmission to another controller (Art. 20 GDPR).
  6. To object to the processing of your personal data, provided that the processing is based on a legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).

5. Right to complain to a supervisory authority

You have the right to complain to a supervisory authority if you believe that the processing of your personal data violates the GDPR. Until incorporation is complete and a registered seat is published, you may contact any data-protection supervisory authority in Germany; once the company seat is known, the authority of that federal state will be responsible.

6. Data security

Access to the website is encrypted via HTTPS (TLS). We do not set cookies and do not use analytics, marketing or other tracking technologies on this site.

7. Changes

We may update this privacy policy to reflect legal requirements or changes to this website. The version published at the time of your visit applies.
